# HR-analytics

📊 People Analytics: Employee Attrition Analysis
📌 Project Overview

Employee attrition is one of the most critical challenges faced by organizations. High turnover rates increase recruitment costs, reduce productivity, and impact overall business performance.

This project analyzes employee attrition patterns to identify the key factors influencing workforce turnover and provide actionable recommendations for improving employee retention.

The analysis was performed using Power BI, DAX, Power Query, and Excel to transform raw HR data into meaningful business insights and interactive dashboards.


🎯 Business Problem

Organizations often struggle to understand:

Why employees leave the company.
Which departments experience the highest attrition.
How compensation impacts employee retention.
Whether overtime contributes to employee turnover.
How job satisfaction influences attrition.
Which employee groups are at greater risk of leaving.

This project aims to answer these questions through data-driven analysis.
